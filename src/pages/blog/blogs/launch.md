Base Dollar is now live on Base. You can borrow BD against supported collateral or deposit BD into a Stability Pool to earn yield.

Base Dollar is a friendly fork of Liquity V2, a collateralized debt position (CDP) protocol. At launch, you can borrow against:
- ETH
- wstETH
- rETH
- cbETH
- cbBTC

We also plan to add AERO and select Aero LP positions as collateral soon after [Aero](https://aero.xyz/) launches later this year.

Borrowers who use these positions will continue earning AERO rewards while they borrow. The interest rate on each loan will also affect its AERO rewards. Positions with above-average rates will receive a proportional boost, while positions with below-average rates will receive fewer rewards. Our docs explain the mechanism in more detail.

## Earn with BD

You can get BD by borrowing through Base Dollar or buying it on a DEX. BD deposited in a Stability Pool earns yield from:
- 75% of borrower fees for the life of the protocol
- 100% of the liquidation fees generated by that Stability Pool's collateral branch
- Additional OEV rewards from our oracle partner, API3

## Security reviews

Before launch, we put the Base Dollar code through two rounds of security review from Sherlock and countless rounds of AI audit scans prior for findings and stress testing, including one from Zero Cool.

The Sherlock auditors deserve a special shout out here. Their incredible thoroughness helped both secure our protocol and save another in the process. During our security reviews, they found a couple highly nuanced vulnerabilities (we have since fixed obviously) that also affected another protocol in production, giving that team the opportunity to address and fix it.

Audits reduce risk but cannot guarantee full elimination of it. Please review the documentation and understand the protocol before depositing collateral or BD.
