[Base Dollar is now live on Base!](https://app.basedollar.org/) Borrow the $BD stablecoin against supported collateral or deposit $BD into a Stability Pool to earn yield.

Base Dollar is the Base chain friendly fork of Liquity V2, a collateralized debt position (CDP) stablecoin protocol. At launch, $BD can be borrowed against:
- ETH
- wstETH
- rETH
- cbETH
- cbBTC

The Base Dollar protocol is designed with maximum synergy. Soon, AERO token and Aero LP positions will be added as collateral. The next version of [Aero](https://aero.xyz/) launches later this year, and is anticipated to have a new token.

Borrowers who use Aero LP positions will continue earning AERO rewards while they borrow. The interest rate on each loan will also affect its AERO rewards. Positions with above-average rates will receive a proportional boost, while positions with below-average rates will receive fewer rewards. Our [docs](https://docs.basedollar.org/docs/user-docs/lp-token-collaterals#aero-distribution) explain the mechanism in more detail.

## Earn with $BD

Mint $BD by borrowing through Base Dollar or buying it on an exchange. $BD deposited in a Stability Pool earns yield from:
- 75% of borrower fees for the life of the protocol
- 100% of the liquidation fees generated by that Stability Pool's collateral branch
- Additional OEV rewards from our oracle partner, API3

## Security Reviews

Before launch, the Base Dollar codebase underwent [two rounds of rigorous security audits from Sherlock](https://docs.basedollar.org/docs/technical-documentation/audits) and countless rounds of AI scans prior for findings and stress testing, including [one paid professional scan from Zero Cool](https://docs.basedollar.org/docs/technical-documentation/audits).

The Sherlock auditors deserve a special shout out here. Their incredible thoroughness helped both secure our protocol and save another in the process. During our security reviews, Sherlock auditors found a highly nuanced vulnerability (we have since addressed obviously) that also affected another protocol in production, giving that team the opportunity to address and fix it. 

Just through building Base Dollar, we have already saved millions of dollars across the Aero Defi Ecosystem.

Audits reduce risk but cannot guarantee full elimination of it. Please review the documentation and understand the protocol before depositing collateral or $BD.
